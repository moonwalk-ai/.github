# Moonwalk

> *Your personal assistant, always standing by.*

Moonwalk is a personal AI agent platform. Set it up once, connect your tools, and
your assistant actually does the work — sends the email, updates the doc, books the
meeting — instead of just answering questions about it.

No tools to master, nothing technical to set up, no hardware to buy or run. Open
Moonwalk on web, iOS, or Android and put it to work.

**Try it:** <https://moonwalk.now>

## What it does

- **Plugs into your tools.** Connect Gmail, Notion, Google Drive, GitHub, Slack,
  your calendar and more in a click — then your assistant works across all of them.
- **Works the real web.** It opens live pages in a built-in browser, reads them, and
  turns what it finds into briefs and answers — not a list of search results.
- **Runs on its own schedule.** Set up recurring jobs — a daily news brief, a
  morning digest — and it runs them 24/7 without you.
- **Remembers and improves.** Memory and skills that build up over time, so it gets
  better at your work the longer you use it.

## What we believe

- **Technology should stay out of sight.** The best tool is the one you never have
  to learn. Moonwalk should feel like magic from the first install.
- **Do the work, don't describe it.** Answers are easy; outcomes are the point. A
  real assistant takes the task off your plate.
- **Truly yours.** Your agent's memory, skills, and conversations are yours alone —
  private, persistent, and distinct to you. Not a shared, anonymous window that
  forgets you the moment you close it.

## Where we're going

Moonwalk is indistinguishable from a really good human assistant. From install to
advanced use, anyone just gets it. And it connects to anything within reach of the
web.
