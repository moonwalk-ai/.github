# Moonwalk

**AI AGENT THAT JUST WORKS**

## Don't sweat the learning. Open it and put it to work.

No tools to master, nothing technical to set up. Open Moonwalk and your assistant
gets to work for you — any time, on any device.

## Plug in your tools. It does the work.

Connect Gmail, Notion, your calendar and more — then it sends the email, updates the
doc, and books the meeting. Actually doing the work, not just answering.

**Try it:** <https://moonwalk.now>

## Built to get things done

- **It works the real web.** Your agent opens real pages in a built-in browser,
  reads them, and turns what it finds into briefs and answers — not just a list of
  search results.
- **Plugged into your tools.** Connect Gmail, Notion, Google Drive, GitHub, Slack
  and more in a click — then your assistant works across all of them.
- **Runs on its own schedule.** Set up recurring jobs — a daily news brief, a
  morning digest — and your assistant runs them on its own, 24/7.

## What we believe

- **Technology should stay out of sight.** The best tool is the one you never have
  to learn. Moonwalk shouldn't be a difficult tool — it should feel like magic, so
  anyone gets it right from the first install.
- **Do the work, don't describe it.** Answers are easy; outcomes are the point. A
  real assistant takes the task off your plate — reaching into your tools and the
  open web to get it done, not just talk about it.
- **Truly yours.** Your agent's memory, skills, and conversations are yours alone —
  private, persistent, and distinct to you. Not a shared, anonymous window that
  forgets you the moment you close it.

## Where we're going

- Moonwalk is indistinguishable from a really good human assistant.
- From install to advanced use, anyone just gets Moonwalk.
- Moonwalk connects to anything within reach of the web.
