# Moonwalk

**DASHBOARDS THAT RUN THEMSELVES**

## Say what you want to track. It builds the dashboard.

Moonwalk turns what you care about into a live dashboard: your spending, your
habits, your team's work, the news you follow. Ask the AI for one and it lays
out the charts and lists, wires in your data, and keeps everything up to date
on its own.

**Learn more:** https://about.moonwalk.now

## Built to stay current

- **Live widgets.** Charts, kanban boards, calendars and counters you can
  click and edit, not screenshots of numbers.
- **Automations feed them.** Pull from Gmail, Slack, Google Drive or any API
  on a schedule or when something happens. The dashboard updates while
  you're away.
- **Real tables underneath.** Every number comes from rows you own, so one
  table can drive a chart, a list and a total at once.

## Start from something that works

- **Templates.** A finance tracker, a study planner, something just for fun.
  It's in your base in a click and already working. Then change whatever you
  like.
- **Ask AI for changes.** Every dashboard ends with a button. Describe the
  widget to add or the thing to change, and the AI edits it while you watch.
- **Bring your own AI.** Use Claude Code or Codex in the chat on your own
  subscription, or reach your dashboards from any MCP app.
